Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about eligibility, entrance exams, fees, scholarships, and campus life.

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories) from a recognized university is required. Final year undergraduate students are also eligible to apply provisionally.

We accept scores from national exams including CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, and GMAT. Candidates can also appear for the JNU University Entrance Test (JNUET) conducted specifically for MBA admissions.

Yes, reservations and relaxation in qualifying marks are strictly provided as per the statutory norms prescribed by the Government of India for OBC, SC, ST, EWS, and PwD categories.

The total program fee is approximately ₹4.5–5.5 lakh for two years, inclusive of academic, laboratory, and library charges. Merit scholarships and education loan tie-ups with leading banks are readily available.

Yes, JNU provides separate, well-furnished hostel facilities for boys and girls with hygienic mess catering, high-speed Wi-Fi, indoor recreation, and 24×7 security on our green, sprawling campus.

JNU offers high-demand specializations in Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management, Operations & Supply Chain, Business Analytics, and International Business with industry-recognized certifications.

Our dedicated Corporate Relations and Placement Cell provides 100% placement support, executive mentorship, summer internships, and campus recruitment with 100+ premier recruiters like Deloitte, EY, PwC, KPMG, and HSBC.
